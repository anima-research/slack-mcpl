- Cache a channel only once the host accepts its descriptor in `channels/register`'s itemized answer (`accepted: true`), so `refresh_channels` can recover a refused, unanswered or timed-out registration. A descriptor the host refuses (`accepted: false`) is dropped even if an earlier answer accepted it, and closed if it was open. Each answer is logged once if it refuses or leaves out any channel, naming them with the host's reasons, and an answer without itemized results is logged as accepting nothing.
- Discard stale listings and acknowledgements after a registration re-grant, and a `refresh_channels` listing that finishes after its host connection has closed.
- `channels/open` that names a channel, by `channelId` or by its conversation's `address.channelId`, opens that channel or fails with "No matching channel found", rather than opening the first registered channel of the type.
- Refuse `state/rollback`, with `success: false` and a reason, while `slack.messaging` isn't active on the asking connection: before its initial policy, or when its grant doesn't cover that feature set. This includes a TCP client that reconnects with an earlier checkpoint.
