- `send_message`, `reply_message`, `send_dm`, `edit_message` and `channels/publish` now refuse text holding a lone UTF-16 surrogate before Slack is asked, as `upload_file` already did, with "Nothing was posted", "Nothing was sent" or "The message wasn't changed" and what to do. Such text used to reach Slack with the surrogate and the character after it turned into one different character, while the write answered success, or, with the surrogate at the very end, failed with the bare text "URI malformed".
